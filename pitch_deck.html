Signal Architecture

Six Layers of Alpha,
One Accountable Score

Layer 1 — EOD

Technical Signal Scoring

Golden cross (+18), MACD crossover (+12), DMA positioning (+12), delivery spike (+8), institutional bulk deals (+18). Score built from up to 30 independently fired signals.

Layer 2 — EOD

Entry-Timing Guards

Mean-reversion penalties dock overbought names: RSI>80 → −15 pts; 3-month return >35% → −8 pts; price >30% above 50DMA → −12 pts. Calibrated from IC backtest across 156,000 rows.

Layer 3 — Intraday

Live Price / Volume Signals

Refreshed 4× daily. Volume shock ≥5x average → +28 pts; within 0.5% of 52-week high → +22 pts; 20-day breakout → +15 pts. Captures momentum shifts that EOD misses.

Layer 4 — ML Blend

Forward-Return Model

LightGBM trained on 125,000+ labeled rows (500 stocks × 2+ years). Blended in only when walk-forward OOS AUC ≥ 0.52 — the blend weight scales with measured edge. Never blends noise.

Layer 5 — Regime

Adaptive Market Filter

India VIX, breadth %, FII 5-day flows, and Nifty 3M return vote to classify risk-on / neutral / risk-off. In risk-off, weak names (below 200DMA) lose 12 pts — concentrating picks in resilient leaders.

Layer 6 — Portfolio

Sector Cap + Smoothing

Top-50 window capped at 30% per sector (prevents one-sector reversals from taking down the list). EMA smoothing prevents rank churn. Incumbent hysteresis rewards consistency.
